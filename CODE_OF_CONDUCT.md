# Code of Conduct - Nexus Guardian D7D

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, with special emphasis on
creating a safe environment for discussions about child protection and education.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing on what is best for the community and child protection mission
* Showing empathy towards other community members
* Prioritizing child safety in all discussions and contributions

### Unacceptable Behavior

* Sexualized language or imagery of any kind involving minors
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Any attempt to circumvent or weaken child protection features
* Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional or child-protection setting

## Child Protection Principles

All community members must adhere to:

1. **Zero Tolerance**: No tolerance for any content, discussion, or code that
   could harm children
2. **Mandatory Reporting**: Suspicious activities must be reported to
   maintainers immediately
3. **Privacy First**: Children's privacy and data protection is paramount
4. **Transparency**: All AI decisions affecting children must be explainable
5. **Ethical AI**: All contributions must align with ethical AI principles

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project team. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other
members of the project's leadership.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1,
with additional child protection provisions specific to this project's mission.
